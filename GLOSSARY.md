# Glossary

**Series document.** A short-drama title as stored and shown. Price zero, or an explicit free mark, means free. Anything other than published is a draft.

**Entitlement.** What a checkout mode grants a viewer, and the inverse a refund or a basic plan applies. A weekly pass is 7 days. A subscription pass is 30 days. Grant writes Plus. Revoke writes the basic plan and ends the expiry.

**Playback decision.** Whether a signed-in viewer may play an episode, must sign in, or must pay.

**Revenue report.** Successful payments in a time window, allocated across series and staff. A creator sees only their own rows.

**Publish notice.** The email, SMS, and Telegram notices that follow a published series. Mail and SMS share one claim. Telegram is claimed per episode.
